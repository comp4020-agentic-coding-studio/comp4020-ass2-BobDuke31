# Why would anyone pay to feel bad, on purpose, repeatedly?

---

## The paradox of failure

- a sad film happens **to** you
- a game's failure happens **because of** you
- and somehow that's the part people come back for

— Jesper Juul, *The Art of Failure*

---

## What this course studies

- not bad games — the *design* of losing in good ones
- checkpoints, permadeath, lives, difficulty, ethics, feel
- twelve weeks, one running question

## The shape of the semester

theory → history → mechanics → meaning → social → craft

ending on a capstone: you design a fail state, not just analyze one
