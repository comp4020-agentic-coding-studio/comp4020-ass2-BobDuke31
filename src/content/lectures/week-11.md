Bennett Foddy has said plainly that *Getting Over It* was built to be
unpleasant. It found an enormous audience anyway, because the unpleasantness
is hilarious to watch even when it is miserable to endure. Streaming turned
that gap between player experience and audience experience into its own
economy.

## Outline

- what a rage game optimizes for, if not the player's enjoyment
- the player/audience split, and why some fail states are designed for the
  second party
- what a designer owes an audience that a walkthrough will eventually turn
  their game's failure into
