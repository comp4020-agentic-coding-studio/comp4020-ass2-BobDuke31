Csikszentmihalyi's flow model says engagement lives in a narrow channel
between boredom and anxiety, with challenge tracking skill closely. Soulslikes
appear to break this on purpose, holding difficulty well above the player's
current skill and letting repeated failure close the gap instead. That's
either a counterexample to flow theory, or a second channel it didn't
originally describe.

## Outline

- the classical flow model, briefly, and where it predicts frustration
- "resilient flow": informative, consistent failure as its own kind of
  engagement
- what makes a Soulslike death informative rather than just punishing
- difficulty as a message, not just a dial
