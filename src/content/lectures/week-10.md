Losing a ranked match is one failure. Working out afterward whose fault it
was is a second one, and attribution theory predicts it will go badly: people
reliably rate their own mistakes as circumstantial and their teammates'
mistakes as characteristic. Multiplayer design either manages that tendency
or hands it a scoreboard and gets out of the way.

## Outline

- tilt, defined through attribution rather than just mood
- how post-match UI (scoreboards, replays, MVP systems) shapes who gets blamed
- design choices that measurably reduce blame-seeking after a loss
