"Three lives" is not a design decision about challenge. It's a decision about
revenue per cabinet-hour, made in the 1980s, that quietly became a convention
so durable it outlived every arcade that required it.

## Outline

- how arcade operators priced difficulty by the coin, not the curve
- the home console problem: what do you tune difficulty *for* once the coin
  slot is gone
- lives and continues as vestigial organs — mechanics that survived their
  original purpose
- games that finally removed them, and what replaced the job they were doing
