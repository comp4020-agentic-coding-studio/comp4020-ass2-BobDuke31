A speedrunner resets constantly and experiences almost none of it as failure.
The run wasn't a story that needed a happy ending — it was a measurement.
Understanding why reframes a lot of what "failing" even means once a game is
played competitively against a clock rather than against an ending.

## Outline

- resets as data collection, not narrative collapse
- what's preserved across a reset (route knowledge) versus what a roguelike
  preserves across a death
- how speedrun-friendly design choices (fast restarts, visible timers) quietly
  reduce the sting of failure for everyone, not just runners
