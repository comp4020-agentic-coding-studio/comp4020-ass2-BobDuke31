Nothing about a block breaking requires a screen shake. The logic is
identical with or without one: the block existed, you acted on it, it stopped
existing. "Juice" — the shake, the flash, the momentary pause — changes
nothing about what happened and everything about whether it registered.

## Try it

<LiveDemo mode="juice" />

Both blocks break on the same click, with the identical outcome. Watch how
differently the two announce it.

**Notice:**

- the meter on the juiced block moves further even though the same one thing happened
- how much of "juice" is actually just *timing* — a beat of hesitation before the burst
- whether you can point to the exact frame it stopped feeling like feedback and started feeling like an event

## Outline

- juice as feedback design, separated from the mechanic it's attached to
- why the moment of failure is where juice usually matters most
- what this course has actually been arguing for twelve weeks: that failure
  is designed, at every layer, on purpose
- the final project brief and what a strong fail state needs to demonstrate
