Classical flow theory says challenge and skill should track each other
closely, or a player gets bored or anxious. Soulslikes seem to violate that on
purpose and players love them for it — which is either a hole in flow theory
or evidence that failure itself can be part of the message a difficulty curve
sends.

## Before the session

Bring a difficulty spike from a game you've played that you think was trying
to tell you something, rather than just being hard.

## In the session

We test "resilient flow" — the idea that repeated, informative failure can
itself sustain engagement — against the examples people bring.

Difficulty-as-communication is the last theory week before the course turns
