Every checkpoint is an argument about how much of a mistake a player should
have to repeat. The lecture's live demo puts the same hazard behind two
different policies so the difference stops being theoretical.

## Before the session

Run the demo on the [week 4 lecture page](/lectures/week-04/) a few times
under each policy before you arrive. Notice which one you resent less, and
try to say why in mechanical terms rather than a mood.

## In the session

Group critique of checkpoint placement in games people brought — not "is it
generous," but "generous with what, exactly": time, progress, or context.

## Afterwards

The [checkpoint-design assessment](/assessments/checkpoint-design/) opens
today. This session's critique is where most students find their brief.
