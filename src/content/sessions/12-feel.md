Nothing this semester has argued that failure needs to feel bad. Some of it
has argued the opposite: that how a fail state is presented — the shake, the
flash, the sound — is doing as much work as the mechanic underneath it. This
is the week that argument gets tested directly, and the week your own fail
state gets shown to the room.

## Before the session

Run the juice comparison on the [lecture page](/lectures/week-12/) with
feedback on and off. Bring your final project ready to run in front of the
class.

## In the session

Short live demos of everyone's final project, judged first on whether the
fail state is legible on sight, then on whether it says anything the rest of
the semester hadn't already said better.

## Afterwards

[Final projects](/assessments/final-project/) are due at the end of the
semester. This is the last taught session.
