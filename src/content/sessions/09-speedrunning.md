A speedrunner who resets forty seconds into an attempt has, by every ordinary
measure, just failed. They don't experience it that way. The run was
diagnostic, not a story with a sad ending — and that reframing has quietly
influenced how a lot of modern games are designed to be replayed.

## Before the session

Watch a few minutes of a speedrun with resets left in, and notice the
runner's tone at the moment of reset.

## In the session

We connect this back to week 5's permadeath discussion: what's kept across a
reset (route knowledge) versus what a roguelike keeps across a death, and why
one stings and the other doesn't.
