Three lives, a game over screen, a continue that costs another coin: none of
it was designed around what makes a satisfying challenge. It was designed
around how many quarters a cabinet needed to earn per hour. That constraint
outlived the cabinets.

## Before the session

Find a game — any era — that still has "lives" as a mechanic, and ask what
job that mechanic is actually doing now that nobody's feeding it coins.

## In the session

We trace the arcade economy forward: home consoles removing the coin but
keeping the lives, then games that finally let the mechanic go entirely.
