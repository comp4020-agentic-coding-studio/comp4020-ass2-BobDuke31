Celeste ships a difficulty toggle that widens timing windows and slows the
game down, on request, mid-run, with no penalty. Some players consider that
exactly what a fair game should offer. Others consider it a betrayal of what
the game was for. Both positions are held by people who love the game.

## Before the session

Try the week's live demo on the [lecture page](/lectures/week-08/) with
Assist Mode both on and off, and notice which feels like a different game and
which just feels like the same game with the volume turned down.

## In the session

Structured argument, not a vote: everyone defends the position they disagree
with more than they defend their own, using the demo as shared evidence.
