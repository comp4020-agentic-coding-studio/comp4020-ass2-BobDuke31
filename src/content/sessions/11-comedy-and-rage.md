Bennett Foddy built *Getting Over It* to be genuinely unpleasant to play and
it became one of the most-watched games of its era, because losing at it is
funny to everyone except the person losing. Streamer culture has since built
an entire economy on other people's fail states.

## Before the session

Watch a short clip of someone failing spectacularly at a rage game and pay
attention to what the game does at the exact moment of failure to make that
clip watchable.

## In the session

We separate the player's experience of a fail state from the audience's, and
ask what a designer owes to each — directly relevant if your final project
will ever be watched, not just played.
