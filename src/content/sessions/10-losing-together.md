Losing alone is between you and the game. Losing with four other people
introduces a second failure state on top of the first: working out whose
fault it was. Attribution theory predicts teams will blame teammates more
than themselves, and multiplayer game design either fights that tendency or
feeds it.

## Before the session

Bring a post-match screen, scoreboard, or replay feature from a multiplayer
game and say whether it helps a team figure out what went wrong or just gives
them ammunition.

## In the session

Comparing post-match feedback systems across genres — what gets measured, and
what that measurement quietly implies about who lost the game.
