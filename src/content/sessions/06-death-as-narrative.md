Most games treat death as downtime: a screen you wait through before the game
resumes. *Hades* treats every death as the next beat of the story. That's not
free — it requires the narrative to be built to absorb repetition from the
ground up.

## Before the session

Play or watch enough of a run-based narrative game to see how it handles a
death mid-story, and bring the moment where it either worked or didn't.

## In the session

We push on where the technique breaks: games where narrative death-as-progress
would undercut the stakes it's trying to build, and why.
