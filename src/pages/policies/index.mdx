# Policies and support

Assessment briefs link back here rather than restating these rules, so read
this page once and refer back to it rather than to memory.

## Late work

Work submitted after the deadline loses 5% of the available marks per day,
to a maximum of five days, after which it is marked as a fail state the
course did not design on purpose: not accepted. If a genuine circumstance
gets in the way of a deadline, ask for an extension before the deadline
passes, not after.

## Extensions

Email the convenor before the deadline with what happened and how long you
need. Extensions are granted for illness, misadventure, and similar
disruptions — not for a checkpoint you wish you'd placed earlier in your own
schedule. Short extensions (a few days) are usually approved on request;
longer ones may need supporting documentation.

## Academic integrity

Declare any assistance you used beyond the course's own tools and each
other's feedback in session — an AI assistant, a collaborator, a published
source you drew from directly. Undeclared assistance is treated as
misrepresenting the work as entirely your own, which is the one fail state
in this course with no respawn.

## Getting help

For a question about the material: ask in session, or bring it to office
hours. For a question about your own submission: email the person marking
it, named on the [people page](/people/). For anything else, email the
convenor. Nobody on this course expects you to have already worked it out
alone before asking.
